Exercise: Professions

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 82 of 96 (book page 78).

Exercise

Question 1

Read each sentence. Is it true or false?

  • 1. The police protect us. (True / False)
  • 2. A person who repairs cars is called a mason. (True / False)
  • 3. The school principal runs the school. (True / False)
  • 4. A sweet-maker sells vegetables. (True / False)

Question 2

What is the person who flies an aeroplane called, and how does he help us?

  • 1. First write: what is the person who flies an aeroplane called?
  • 2. Then write: how does he help us?

Question 3

If the car breaks down during a journey, who will you call to get it repaired?

  • 1. Remember the second sentence of Question 1: what is a person who repairs cars called?
  • 2. Write a full sentence.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: The answers are in the pictures on the earlier pages: look again at the police and pilot photos. For a wrong sentence, think who really does that work.
  • Movement: Use the big buttons for the tick and the cross, or show a thumbs up for true and a thumbs down for false. Answers that need writing can be spoken instead.
  • Hearing: Show the new words (mason, sweet-maker, principal, pilot) with pictures and signs so the meaning is clear.
  • For teachers: “Mason” and “sweet-maker” are new words for many children. First explain them with a picture or real object (a brick, a sweet), then do the question. Accept spoken and drawn answers.

Answers

Question 1

  • 1. True
  • 2. False
  • 3. True
  • 4. False

Question 2

  • Sample answer: The person who flies an aeroplane is called a pilot. He takes us quickly and safely from one place to another by aeroplane.

Question 3

  • Sample answer: If the car breaks down during a journey, we will call a mechanic to repair it.
