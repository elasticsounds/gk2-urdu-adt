Exercise: Religious festivals

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 29 of 96 (book page 25).

Activity

With the help of your teachers or parents, collect information about the festivals of other religions and tell your friends in class.

  • 1. Choose one festival, for example Christmas, Holi, Baisakhi or Diwali.
  • 2. Ask your teacher or your mother or father three things: Who celebrates it? When do they celebrate it? What do they do on that day?
  • 3. Remember the answers, write them down or draw a picture.
  • 4. Tell your friends in class.

Exercise

Question 1

How do Muslims celebrate Eid-ul-Fitr?

Question 2

Choose the right name.

1. Eid-ul-Adha is celebrated in memory of the sacrifice of ___.

  • a) Hazrat Isa (peace be upon him)
  • b) Guru Nanak
  • c) Hazrat Ibrahim (peace be upon him)

Question 3

Write the names of any two festivals of other religions.

Question 4

These are riddles! Read the three clues and say which festival or month they are about.

First list:

  • 1. Hazrat Ibrahim (peace be upon him), 10th of Zil-Hajj, sacrifice
  • 2. The moon of the 1st of Shawwal, Fitrana, Eidi
  • 3. Fast, Iftar, blessed month
  • 4. December, Hazrat Isa (peace be upon him), Christians
  • 5. Hindus, throwing colours, happiness
  • 6. First day of the new year, Sikhs, celebrations

Second list:

  • Baisakhi
  • Christmas
  • Eid-ul-Adha
  • Eid-ul-Fitr

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: If you cannot remember an answer, look at the earlier pages again: Eid-ul-Fitr (page 22), Eid-ul-Adha and Milad-un-Nabi (page 23), Christmas, Holi and Baisakhi (page 24). In Question 4, the three words on each line are clues to the same festival.
  • Movement: Instead of writing, the child can say the answer, point to the right answer, or put cards with the names of the festivals in the boxes.
  • Understanding: In Question 4, show one line at a time and cover the rest. At first, let the child choose between just two festival names.
  • For teachers: Let the children work in pairs for the activity. They can share their information by speaking, with a picture or with signs. Remind them that we talk about everyone's festivals with respect.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: Diwali is a festival of Hindus. It is called the festival of lights. On this day people light lamps in their homes and share sweets.

Question 1

  • Sample answer: Muslims celebrate Eid-ul-Fitr on the 1st of Shawwal, after the fasts of the holy month of Ramadan are complete. They give Fitrana before the Eid prayer, offer the Eid prayer, wish their relatives and friends a happy Eid, visit their homes and give Eidi to the children.

Question 2

  • 1. c) Hazrat Ibrahim (peace be upon him)

Question 3

  • Sample answer: Christmas and Holi. (Baisakhi and Diwali are also correct answers.)

Question 4

  • 1. Hazrat Ibrahim (peace be upon him), 10th of Zil-Hajj, sacrifice: Eid-ul-Adha
  • 2. The moon of the 1st of Shawwal, Fitrana, Eidi: Eid-ul-Fitr
  • 3. Fast, Iftar, blessed month: The holy month of Ramadan
  • 4. December, Hazrat Isa (peace be upon him), Christians: Christmas
  • 5. Hindus, throwing colours, happiness: Holi
  • 6. First day of the new year, Sikhs, celebrations: Baisakhi
