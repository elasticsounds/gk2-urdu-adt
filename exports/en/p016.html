The difference between village life and city life

City life

Picture: A very tall city building with many floors, shining with lights in the evening.

Caption: Tall buildings

Picture: An office where many people sit at desks working on computers.

Caption: Office

Village life

Picture: Small mud houses in a village, with a rope bed in the yard and date palm trees behind.

Caption: Mud houses

Picture: A farmer is ploughing a field with a tractor; there are green trees and white birds nearby.

Caption: Farming

Activity

In groups, children compare the main features of a village and a city using the pictures.

  • 1. Sit with two or three friends.
  • 2. Look at the two pictures of homes: mud houses and tall buildings. Say what is different.
  • 3. Look at the two pictures of work: farming and an office. Say what is different.
  • 4. Tell the whole class one thing your group found.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at the pictures in pairs: the top two pictures are places to live, and the bottom two are places to work. The city is on the right and the village is on the left.
  • Vision: Listen to the descriptions of the pictures. Then touch a lump of clay and a baked brick, and listen to the sounds of a tractor and a computer keyboard and say which is which.
  • For teachers: Give each child in the group a job: one puts a finger on a picture, one says a difference, one shows it with a gesture (tall or small). A child who cannot speak can put picture cards into a "village" box or a "city" box.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: In villages, houses are small and made of mud; in cities, buildings are tall and solid. In villages people farm; in cities many people work in offices.
