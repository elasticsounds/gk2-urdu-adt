Flowering and non-flowering plants

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 49 of 96 (book page 45).

Some plants are flowering and some are non-flowering.

Picture: A rose plant with many red flowers in bloom.

Caption: Flowering plants

Picture: A green fern plant with long, feather-like leaves and no flowers.

Caption: Non-flowering plants

Flowering plants have flowers, fruits and seeds. But non-flowering plants do not make flowers or seeds.

Activity

Look carefully at the world around you. Which plants are flowering and which are non-flowering? Write their names with your teacher's help.

  • 1. Go to the school courtyard or garden with your teacher.
  • 2. Look carefully at each plant: does it have flowers, or do flowers grow on it?
  • 3. Make two boxes: flowering and non-flowering. With your teacher's help, write the names of the plants in the right box.

Picture: Many fruits placed together: bananas, a melon, a pineapple, a watermelon, apples, a pear and cherries.

Fruits are an important part of plants. We love to eat fruit. Most fruits have seeds. Some fruits have no seeds.

Tell us

Which fruit do you love to eat?

  • 1. Say the name of your favourite fruit, draw it, or point to it in the picture.
  • 2. Tell us: what colour is it? How does it taste?
  • 3. Think: does this fruit have seeds inside?

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: It is easy to tell: look carefully at the plant. If flowers grow on it, it is flowering. If flowers never grow on it, like a fern, it is non-flowering.
  • Vision: You can know a flower by its smell and by touching its soft petals. Cut a fruit open and feel its seeds with your fingers.
  • Hearing: Agree on two signs: for "flowering" open your fingers like a flower; for "non-flowering" keep your fist closed. Look at each plant and answer with the sign.
  • For teachers: Write the plant names on the board so that the children can copy them. Children who cannot write may answer by drawing the plant or by standing next to it. Cut open a fruit, for example an apple, and let all the children see and touch the seeds.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: Flowering: rose, sunflower, jasmine, mango tree. Non-flowering: fern, moss.

Tell us

  • Sample answer: I like mangoes. A mango is yellow and sweet. It has one big seed, called a stone, inside.
