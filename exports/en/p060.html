Products made from crops: wheat and cotton

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 60 of 96 (book page 56).

Pakistan is an agricultural country. Its important crops include wheat, rice, cotton, sugarcane and maize.

Most of the people live in villages and work in farming. Many people also keep livestock.

Try it

How is roti made? Put the journey from the field to the table in the right order.

Put these in the right order:

  • Flour is made from wheat.
  • Roti is made from flour.
  • The farmer grows wheat.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: On the printed page, look at the pictures from right to left like a story: first the field, then the mill, then the tandoor. Count the five crops on your fingers: wheat, rice, cotton, sugarcane, maize.
  • Vision: Hold some grains of wheat, some flour and a piece of roti in your hand and feel them. In the same way, touch a cotton boll or some cotton wool and feel how soft it is.
  • Hearing: Some words look the same on the lips, like "flour" and "flower". Make the difference clear by writing both words and showing the real things.
  • For teachers: Make three cards (wheat, flour, roti) and ask the children to put them in order. Children can answer by placing the cards, by pointing or by speaking.
