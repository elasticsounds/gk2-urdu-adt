Chapter 14: Respecting others

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Recognise that all human beings are equal and important in terms of rights.
  • Know that all human beings are alike, but differ in family, religion, culture and so on, and that all deserve respect.
  • Recognise that all human beings are equal by birth and deserve honour and respect.
  • Identify ways in which you can respect everyone.
  • Identify occasions when waiting for your turn is very necessary (while talking, while buying a ticket, at the school canteen, etc.).
  • Understand that it is necessary to respect the needs, interests, ideas and feelings of others.

Picture: A smiling little girl is lovingly hugging her friend.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: This chapter is about “respect”. Think: who did you speak politely to today? Greeting, saying thank you and waiting for your turn are all respect.
  • Understanding: Do not read all six points at once. Remember just one idea: all people are equal and we respect everyone.
  • Hearing: Learn the signs for “respect” and “equal” and use them again and again through the chapter.
  • For teachers: Read the outcomes in child-friendly words (“We will learn that…”). Make a circle where every child — a wheelchair user, a child who is hard of hearing, a quiet child — sits in an equal place and each gets a turn.
