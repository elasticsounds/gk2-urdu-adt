Activity

Put each animal in the right box: land, water, or both.

Groups: Land animals (red); Water animals (blue); Animals of both land and water (green).

Items:

  • Camel
  • Crocodile
  • Elephant
  • Fish
  • Lion

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Remember: red is for land, blue is for water and green is for both. A crocodile swims in water and also lies in the sun on the bank.
  • Vision: From right to left, the five pictures are: camel, crocodile, fish, lion, elephant. Instead of colouring, you can say the answer aloud: land, water or both.
  • Movement: If colouring is hard, put a red, blue or green sticker or bottle top on the box, or point with your finger.
  • For teachers: Put water in one bowl and soil in another. The children put toy animals or animal cards next to the right bowl. Animals that live in both places go in the middle.
