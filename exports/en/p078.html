Depending on one another

In daily life people depend on one another. To get the electricity in our home repaired, we need the services of an electrician. In the same way, we need a plumber to fit a water tap, and labourers and craftsmen to build a house.

Picture: Two men sit beside a water pump joining a pipe. This is a plumber's work.

Picture: Labourers and masons stand on bamboo scaffolding building a brick wall.

Picture: A workman in a cap repairs the wires of an electrical machine with a tool while two people watch.

Exercise

Question 1

Match the first part of each sentence with its correct second part.

First list:

  • 1. When we feel unwell,
  • 2. Helping others
  • 3. By living together,
  • 4. To build a house,

Second list:

  • is a good habit.
  • we get help from a doctor.
  • we grow closer.
  • we need a labourer's help.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: For each photo ask: who is this and what work do they do for us? In the matching question, first match the pair you are sure about.
  • Vision: Listen to the photo descriptions: a plumber joins a pipe, labourers build a brick wall, a workman repairs an electrical machine. At home, feel a tap and a light switch with your hands.
  • Understanding: Match only one pair at a time. Remember: sick — doctor, house — labourer.
  • For teachers: Play “Who helps?”: you name a problem (the tap is dripping) and children say the helper's name, hold up a picture card or mime the job.

Answers

Question 1

  • 1. When we feel unwell,: we get help from a doctor.
  • 2. Helping others: is a good habit.
  • 3. By living together,: we grow closer.
  • 4. To build a house,: we need a labourer's help.
