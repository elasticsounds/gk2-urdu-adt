Chapter 7: Plants

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Identify the different parts of a plant (root, stem, leaf and flower) and say what each part does.
  • Identify the different shapes of leaves.
  • Identify the roots that people eat.
  • Name the flowering and non-flowering plants around you.
  • Identify which fruits have seeds in them.
  • Recognise that some plants grow from seeds, some from roots and some from stems.
  • Identify that soil, water, air and light are needed for a plant to grow.
  • Explain the importance of plants in climate change.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Before reading, think: which plant or tree is near your home or school? Tell your partner its name.
  • Understanding: Do not try to remember all eight points at once. Just remember four words: root, stem, leaf, flower. Raise one finger for each word.
  • Vision: Hold a real small plant. Feel it from bottom to top: root, stem, leaf, flower.
  • For teachers: Before starting the chapter, bring a real plant, with its roots, to class. Let every child see, touch and smell it. Children who cannot speak can answer by pointing to the part of the plant.
