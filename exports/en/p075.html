The question from the previous page continues. Put each thing in the right box: a natural source or made by people?

Question 3

Draw pictures of any two natural sources of light and also write their names. Name ______ Name ______

  • 1. Think: what are the natural sources of light? (See page 67.)
  • 2. Draw two boxes and draw one source in each box.
  • 3. Write its name under each picture.

Question 4

Why is the sun important for plants? Write two reasons.

  • 1. Look at the sunflower picture on page 69 and the sentence under it.
  • 2. Remember: which two things does the sun give us?
  • 3. Write two reasons, say them, or show them in a drawing.

Question 5

Which sources of heat are used in your home for making food? Write their names.

  • 1. Remember your kitchen at home, or ask your parents.
  • 2. Write, say or draw the things on which food is cooked or heated.

For teachers: Tell the children the difference between heat and light, and their uses.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look back to find the answers: natural sources are on page 67, and the sun and plants are on page 69.
  • Vision: In Question 3, instead of drawing, model a round sun, a crescent moon or a star from clay or dough, or make a raised-line picture with thick string or glue, and say the names aloud.
  • Movement: If drawing or writing is hard, choose and paste two natural sources from ready-made pictures, and dictate your answers to Questions 4 and 5 to a buddy or the teacher.
  • For teachers: In Question 5 every home's answer will differ (gas, wood, dung cakes, coal, electricity) — accept all answers respectfully and allow no comparing or teasing about any child's home. Oral, drawn and signed answers deserve full credit too.

Answers

Question 2 (continued)

  • Natural source: Stars
  • Made by people: Heater; Candle

Question 3

  • Sample answer: The sun, the moon and the stars are natural sources of light. Draw any two of them, for example: Name: sun — Name: moon.

Question 4

  • Sample answer: (1) Sunlight is necessary for the growth of plants and crops. (2) The sun gives plants heat; without light and heat plants cannot grow well.

Question 5

  • Sample answer: Each home may have a different answer, for example: gas stove, wood fire (clay stove or tandoor), electric heater or oven, coal brazier.
