How does water reach our homes?

The water used in homes comes from two sources.

Under the ground: wells, tube wells and so on.

On the surface of the ground: springs, waterfalls, streams, rivers, lakes and so on.

Surface water is cleaned at a filtration plant and then reaches our homes through a pipeline.

Picture: In this diagram there is a big blue water tank on the left. A pipe runs from it to three filter tanks and then to two small blue filters. From there the clean water travels through a pipeline to a house on the right.

Do you know? Filtering and boiling water are ways of cleaning water. Children! Have you seen a filtration plant anywhere near you? With such a filtration plant we clean a large amount of water.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Follow the diagram with your finger from left to right: tank, then filters, then pipe, then house. Then think: where does the water in your home come from? From a hand pump, a motor or a pipeline?
  • Vision: Make the route on a table with string or a piece of pipe: a bowl for the tank at one end, a sieve for the filter in the middle and a box for the house at the other end. Trace the route with a finger and describe the journey of water.
  • Understanding: Read what the two children say, one at a time. First the boy: where water comes from. Then the girl: how water is cleaned and reaches our homes.
  • For teachers: Filter muddy water through muslin or a sieve to show what 'filtering' means; only adults should do the boiling. If there is a filtration plant nearby, show it to the children or bring a photo. Use hand-down and hand-flat gestures for 'underground' and 'surface'.
