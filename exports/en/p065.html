Exercise: Milk, hens and roti

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 65 of 96 (book page 61).

Question 4

Question 4. Which thing made from milk do you love to eat, and why?

  • 1. Remember the things made from milk: yoghurt, butter, cheese and ghee.
  • 2. Choose your favourite one.
  • 3. Write or say why you like it.

Question 5

Question 5. What do we get from the hen? (i) ______ (ii) ______

  • 1. Think: what do we get from the hen and eat at breakfast?
  • 2. Write or say the names of two things.

Question 6

Look at the picture of the roti and choose the right crop.

1. Which crop is the roti we eat at home made from?

  • a) Sugarcane
  • b) Cotton
  • c) Wheat

For teachers: Show children pictures of different crops and their seeds. Also explain the importance of these crops.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In Question 4 no answer is wrong; say what you like and give a reason using "because". For Question 6, remember the pictures on page 56.
  • Movement: If writing a long answer is hard, say it aloud and let a grown-up write it, or record your voice.
  • Hearing: Use pictures or real objects to understand the questions: a bowl of yoghurt, an egg, a roti. Answers can also be given by sign or by choosing a picture.
  • For teachers: Some children do not eat dairy or eggs (allergy or family practice). Ask them what their family members like. Accept a drawing, a single word or a full sentence as an answer.

Answers

Question 4

  • Sample answer: I like yoghurt very much because it is tasty and cool, and it is good for my health.

Question 6

  • 1. c) Wheat
