Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 10 of 96 (book page 6).

Do you know? Malam Jabba and Lake Saif-ul-Malook are very important places for tourism.

Picture: Bab-e-Khyber: a big gateway built of stone, with a round tower on each side and an arch over the road in the middle. The flag of Pakistan is flying on top.

Caption: Bab-e-Khyber

The people here speak the Pashto, Hindko, Saraiki, Khowar and Kohistani languages. Men's dress is shalwar qameez, a turban, a Chitrali cap, a karakul cap and Peshawari sandals. Women wear an embroidered qameez and shalwar.

The famous foods here are pulao and chapli kabab. People also love to drink green tea.

Picture: Two round, flat chapli kababs on a plate, with slices of tomato on top and a piece of lemon beside them.

Do you know? "Attan" and "Khattak" are the famous dances here.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Three things about Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: the language is Pashto, the dress is the Chitrali cap and Peshawari sandals, and the food is pulao and chapli kabab. Remember from the last page: its capital is Peshawar.
  • Vision: Listen to the picture descriptions. To understand the shape of Bab-e-Khyber, stand two glasses up and lay a book on top of them: two towers with a gate in between.
  • Hearing: Show the names of the languages (Pashto, Hindko, Khowar) written on cards. A video of the 'Attan' and 'Khattak' dances can be watched and understood even without sound.
  • For teachers: If a child in the class speaks Pashto or Hindko, ask them to teach everyone 'hello' or 'thank you' in their language. This builds respect for every child's language.
