What problems arise from wasting water, and what are the ways to prevent them?

Picture: A teacher is teaching children under the trees. The blackboard has two columns: "Problems" and "Ways to prevent them". The children are giving their answers in turn.

Problems

  • Everyday jobs come to a stop.
  • Crops are destroyed.
  • A shortage of drinking water.
  • More diseases.

Ways to prevent them

  • Keeping water safe from pollution.
  • Storing water.
  • Using it carefully and not wasting it.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: First look at the four pictures and find where the water is flowing in each. Then think: does this happen in my home too?
  • Hearing: In the picture of the teacher, the children's words are written in bubbles: pink bubbles are problems and yellow bubbles are ways to prevent them. Use the colours to tell them apart.
  • Movement: If dragging is hard, just say or point to show whether an idea is a "problem" or "prevention"; a partner can place it in the column.
  • For teachers: Give a demonstration: one child washes their hands with a mug of water, and another under a running tap (or a pouring bottle). Collect the used water in two separate bowls and compare. Blind children can feel the difference by lifting the bowls.
