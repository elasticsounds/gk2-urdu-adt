Chapter 4: Religious festivals

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Know why and how Muslims celebrate Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha.
  • Know about the religious and cultural festivals of other religions that are celebrated in Pakistan.

At a festival people celebrate and share in the happiness of others. Muslims celebrate two Eids in a year with great enthusiasm: Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha.

People of other religions living in Pakistan also celebrate their own religious and cultural festivals, for example Christmas, Baisakhi, Diwali and Holi.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Think: which festival is celebrated in your home? What do you wear and eat on that day? Remember it, then read the lesson.
  • Understanding: Count the six festival names one by one on your fingers: Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, Christmas, Baisakhi, Diwali, Holi.
  • Hearing: Agree on a sign for each festival, for example a hug for Eid, throwing colours for Holi, and a tree shape for Christmas.
  • For teachers: Your class may include children of every religion. Give each child a chance to talk about their own festival (by speaking, drawing or using signs), and speak of all festivals with equal respect.
