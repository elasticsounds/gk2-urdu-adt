Different professions

Look at the given pictures to see which professions people take up to earn a living.

Picture: Two women pilots in uniform sit in an aeroplane cockpit in front of buttons and dials.

Caption: Pilot

Picture: At an open-air washing place, a man stands in the water and washes a cloth by beating it on a stone.

Caption: Washerman

Picture: In a classroom a teacher stands by the blackboard teaching while children sit at desks.

Caption: Teacher

Picture: A traffic policeman in uniform, cap and mask raises a white-gloved hand to stop the motorcycles.

Caption: Police

All these people have an important role in our daily life and we should be thankful to them.

Activity

What would you like to become when you grow up, and why?

  • 1. Think: which profession do you like best?
  • 2. Say: “When I grow up I will be a ___.”
  • 3. Say: “Because ___.” Who will you help through this profession?

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In each photo look at the clothes and the place: uniform, cockpit, blackboard, washing place. These make it easy to recognise the profession.
  • Vision: Listen to the descriptions of all four photos. Play a game of recognising professions by sound: an aeroplane, a whistle, clothes being washed, a school bell.
  • Understanding: Choose between two: “Do you want to be a doctor or a teacher?” Pointing to a picture card is also an answer.
  • For teachers: Give every child a turn to share a favourite profession by speaking, signing, drawing or miming. Encourage boys, girls and children with disabilities towards every profession — the pilots in the photo are women.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: When I grow up I will be a teacher because I like teaching children.
