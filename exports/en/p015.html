Chapter 2: Villages and cities

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Recognise that the people of Pakistan live in villages and cities.
  • Identify the main features of villages (buildings, facilities, environment and occupations).
  • Point out the main features of a city.
  • Compare village life and city life.
  • Learn about the occupations that people in cities and villages have in common.

Picture: A village: mud houses, trees, and cows and buffaloes tied in an open yard.

Picture: A city: wide roads and bridges curving over and under each other, with many vehicles on them and many buildings all around.

Most of Pakistan's people live in villages. In villages, houses are usually open and made of mud, and the environment is clean. There are few health and education facilities.

In a city, buildings are tall and solid, and the environment is not clean because of the factories and the noise of traffic. Cities have the best health and education facilities.

Try it

Read or listen to the paragraphs above. Then say: is this about the village or the city?

Groups: Village; City.

Items:

  • Buildings are tall and solid
  • Health and education facilities are few
  • Health and education facilities are the best
  • Houses are open and made of mud
  • The environment is clean
  • There is noise from factories and traffic

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at the two pictures first: the village is on top, the city below. Think: which of these places is your own home like?
  • Vision: A way to remember: a village has mud houses, open space and quiet. A city has tall, solid buildings and the noise of vehicles. Touch some clay and a brick to feel the difference between a mud house and a solid house.
  • For teachers: Read the learning outcomes one at a time in simple words. Ask the children who lives in a village and who lives in a city; children who cannot speak can answer by pointing to a picture.
