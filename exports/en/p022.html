The rights of citizens

It is the duty of the government to provide its citizens with their basic facilities.

It is the duty of a responsible citizen to respect all institutions.

Try it

The government has made an institution for each need. Match the need with the right institution.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Under each picture two things are written: the need and the place, like "Hospitals for treatment". For each picture ask: "What is this place for?"
  • Vision: Listen to the descriptions of the pictures, then count the five institutions on your fingers: hospital, school, police station, court, road. Think of a sound for each one (an ambulance siren, a school bell, a police whistle).
  • Hearing: Write hard words like "elections" and "justice" on the board and explain them with gestures: the gesture for casting a vote, and the gesture for a pair of scales.
  • For teachers: If possible, take the children to visit an institution in the neighbourhood (a dispensary or a post office), or show them pictures of it. Ask each child which of these places they have been to; they may also answer with a gesture or by choosing a picture.
