Try it

These people work in cities and in villages too. Match each occupation with the work it does.

First list:

  • 1. Doctor
  • 2. Tailor
  • 3. Barber
  • 4. Carpenter
  • 5. Motor mechanic
  • 6. Cobbler

Second list:

  • Cuts hair
  • Makes things out of wood
  • Mends shoes
  • Repairs vehicles
  • Sews clothes
  • Treats sick people

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In each picture, look at the tool in the person's hands: a sewing machine, a stethoscope, scissors, wood, car tools, a shoe. The tool helps you remember the occupation.
  • Vision: Recognise each occupation by touching real objects: cloth and thread for the tailor, a shoe for the cobbler, a piece of wood for the carpenter, a comb for the barber, a toy stethoscope for the doctor and a spanner for the mechanic.
  • Hearing: Make a gesture for each occupation: sewing, snipping with scissors, hammering, feeling a pulse. Children look at the gesture and find the picture.
  • For teachers: Tell the children that every occupation deserves respect and that both men and women can do these jobs. Ask which of these people work in their own neighbourhood.
