Exercise: Protecting the earth's resources

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 70 of 96 (book page 66).

Exercise

Question 1

Look at each picture. If it is a good thing choose the happy face; if it is a bad thing choose the sad face.

Groups: Happy face (good thing); Sad face (bad thing).

Items:

  • A running tap with water gushing out
  • Clean water kept covered
  • Factory smoke and dirty water
  • Many green trees

Question 2

Choose the right word for the blank in each sentence.

1. Clean ___ is used for drinking.

  • a) home
  • b) water
  • c) waste
  • d) weather

2. Forests are the ___ of birds and animals.

  • a) home
  • b) water
  • c) waste
  • d) weather

3. Planting more trees keeps the ___ pleasant.

  • a) home
  • b) water
  • c) waste
  • d) weather

4. We should not ___ water.

  • a) home
  • b) water
  • c) waste
  • d) weather

Question 3

Write any three reasons why water is wasted in your area. (i) ______ (ii) ______ (iii) ______

  • 1. Look again at the four pictures on page 63.
  • 2. Think which of these things happen in your area.
  • 3. Write, say or draw three reasons.

Question 4

Why is cutting down forests harmful? Write two reasons. (i) ______ (ii) ______

  • 1. Read or listen again to the text on page 64.
  • 2. Find out what forests do for us.
  • 3. Think about what will happen if the forests are gone, and write two reasons.

For teachers: Tell the children why cutting down forests is dangerous for us. Make the children aware of the idea of SOS (Save Our Soul).

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: If you cannot remember an answer, go back: the water topics are on page 63 and the forest topics are on pages 64 and 65.
  • Vision: The four pictures in Question 1 are: (1) a running tap with water pouring out, (2) many green trees, (3) factory smoke and dirty water, (4) a covered glass and cup of clean water. Listen and say whether each one is good or bad.
  • Movement: For Questions 3 and 4, you may say your answers and have them recorded, or ask a partner to write them down. In Question 1, show thumbs up or thumbs down instead of drawing a circle.
  • For teachers: Accept oral, drawn or signed answers for the writing questions. Make word cards for the fill-in words and hand them to children; show children with intellectual difficulties only two cards at a time and give extra time.

Answers

Question 1

  • Happy face (good thing): Many green trees; Clean water kept covered
  • Sad face (bad thing): A running tap with water gushing out; Factory smoke and dirty water

Question 2

  • 1. b) water
  • 2. a) home
  • 3. d) weather
  • 4. c) waste

Question 3

  • Sample answer: (i) Leaving the tap running while washing your face and hands. (ii) The motor still running after the tank is full. (iii) A broken pipeline. (Also: washing the car or the floor with a hose, which is an unnecessary use of water.)

Question 4

  • Sample answer: (i) Forests are the home of animals and birds; cutting forests down destroys that home. (ii) Forests stop the soil from being washed away and protect us from floods; when forests are cut down, more soil is washed away and there are more floods. (Also: the weather does not stay pleasant and there is less wood.)
