Exercise: Natural environment and resources

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 35 of 96 (book page 31).

Activity

(Group work) Make a list of the natural resources and the things made by people that are in your school.

  • 1. Draw two boxes on a sheet of paper: “Natural resources” and “Things made by people”.
  • 2. With your group, look at the things in the classroom and the courtyard.
  • 3. Write the name of each thing in the right box, or draw a picture of it.
  • 4. Show your list to the class.

Exercise

Question 1

There are five things in the picture. Find the living things (on the printed page, colour them in).

Groups: Living (colour it in); Non-living (do not colour it in).

Items:

  • Football
  • Guitar
  • Insect (beetle)
  • Tree
  • Zebra

Picture: There are five black outline pictures to colour in: an insect (a beetle) on the left, a zebra in the middle, a guitar at the top, a football at the bottom and a tree on the right.

Question 2

Match each natural resource to its use.

First list:

  • 1. Air
  • 2. Water
  • 3. Forest
  • 4. Sun

Second list:

  • Breathing
  • Drinking
  • Light
  • Wood

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In Question 1, ask each picture: “Does it grow? Does it need food and water?” If the answer is yes, colour it in. In Question 2, make the easy pair first (water and drinking). Then the rest will become easy.
  • Vision: Put glue or thread along the outlines of the colouring pictures to raise them, so that the child can feel the shapes. Or give real things such as a toy animal, a leaf and a ball, and ask the child to sort them.
  • Movement: Instead of careful colouring, it is fine to use a thick crayon or finger paint, or just to put one big mark on each living thing. In Question 2, the child can point to the two words instead of drawing a line.
  • For teachers: In the group work, put children of different abilities in each group and give every child a job (looking, telling, writing or drawing). The list can also be spoken or made of pictures.
