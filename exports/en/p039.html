Do you know? Water is cleaned by boiling and filtering it. We use this clean water for drinking and cooking so that we stay safe from diseases.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Think of the six pictures as things we do from morning to night: washing our face and hands, drinking water, cooking food, washing dishes, washing clothes, and watering the fields.
  • Vision: Hold a real object for each use: a glass, a plate, a cloth, a cooking pot, soap. Touch the object and say what water is used for with it.
  • Movement: If dragging the picture to match is hard, say the picture's number or look at it and point, and a partner will make the match for you.
  • For teachers: Have children act out each use (drinking water, washing dishes, washing clothes) while the others guess. No speech is needed, so every child can join in. Show the difference between boiled water and filtered water using a real muslin cloth.
