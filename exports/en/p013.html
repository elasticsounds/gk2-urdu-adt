Map pieces and exercise

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 13 of 96 (book page 9).

Picture: Six separate coloured pieces of the map of Pakistan: a small, thin, dark pink piece for Azad Jammu and Kashmir, a light pink piece for Sindh, blue for Punjab, green for Gilgit-Baltistan, dark blue for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the biggest piece, in orange, for Balochistan.

Try it

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Here is a way to recognise the pieces: the biggest piece is Balochistan, and the smallest and thinnest is Azad Jammu and Kashmir. For the rest, compare the shapes with the map on page 2. Remember the date: 14 August is Independence Day.
  • Vision: Listen to the description of the colours and shapes of the pieces. Hold pieces cut out of card, feel their edges and put them in order from biggest to smallest.
  • Movement: Instead of drawing a tick (✓), you can touch the correct answer, point to it, or say its number (one, two or three).
  • For teachers: Read the question and all three answers aloud and give the children time to think. Children can also answer by holding up fingers (1, 2 or 3). After a wrong answer, read the first paragraph on page 2 together again.
