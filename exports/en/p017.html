Caption: Very little traffic

Activity

Group work: Children talk about the place where they would most like to live, a city or a village, and give two reasons for their choice.

  • 1. Sit in your group.
  • 2. Say whether you would like to live in a city or in a village.
  • 3. Give two reasons for your choice.
  • 4. Listen carefully to your friends' choices and reasons too.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Pay attention to two things: learning (school and university) and the road (little traffic and lots of traffic). Think: what is the road near your home like?
  • Hearing: To explain "noise", show the long rows of vehicles in the picture and put your hands over your ears; to explain "little traffic", show the empty road and make the sign for quiet.
  • Understanding: In the activity, giving just one reason is enough. Use this sentence: "I like ___ because ___."
  • For teachers: This is an opinion question; city and village are both right answers. Children may answer by speaking, by gesture, by choosing a picture or by drawing. Do not let anyone make fun of the place where a child lives.
