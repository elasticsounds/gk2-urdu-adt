The importance of plants

Plants are useful in many ways. The wood of trees is used to make houses and furniture.

Plants also play an important part in changing the climate. They make our environment beautiful. Trees provide food for birds and animals.

Picture: A beautiful garden with beds of colourful flowers, green trees and a wooden shelter.

Picture: Carved wooden furniture: a long seat like a bench and other pieces.

Do you know? Maize, wheat and watermelon are plants that grow from seeds, but mint and ginger grow from roots. Potatoes and sugarcane grow from stems.

Try it

What does each plant grow from? Put each plant in the right box.

Groups: From a seed; From a root; From a stem.

Items:

  • Ginger
  • Maize
  • Mint
  • Potato
  • Sugarcane
  • Watermelon
  • Wheat

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look around your room: the door, the table, the chair, the pencil. They are all made of wood, and wood comes from trees.
  • Understanding: Learn only one group at a time. First: "maize, wheat, watermelon — from seeds". Say it three times with a clap, then go on to the next group.
  • Vision: Run your hand over a wooden table and then touch the trunk of a tree. Both are wood. Cut a fruit open and count the seeds with your fingers.
  • For teachers: Show real things: grains of wheat or maize, a potato with sprouting eyes, and a sprig of mint with its roots. Let the children put them in three baskets (seed, root, stem). Children who cannot write can answer by placing the object.
