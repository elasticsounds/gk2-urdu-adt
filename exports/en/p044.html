Exercise: Water

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 44 of 96 (book page 40).

Exercise

Question 1

Read or listen to each sentence. If it is right, choose "True". If it is not right, choose "False".

  • 1. We should drink clean water. (True / False)
  • 2. Sea water is sweet. (True / False)
  • 3. A well is a natural source of water. (True / False)
  • 4. All living things need water to stay alive. (True / False)

Question 2

Question 2. What three problems could you face if you could not get water?

  • 1. Remember the pictures on page 35: what do we use water for?
  • 2. Think which of those jobs could not be done without water.
  • 3. Write or say three problems.

Question 3

Question 3. Name any two sources of water in your area. (i) ________ (ii) ________

  • 1. Think: where does the water in your home come from?
  • 2. Look around your area: is there a hand pump, well, tube well, canal, river or filtration plant?
  • 3. Write or say any two sources for (i) and (ii).

Picture: This is an uncoloured outline drawing of the Earth as a globe. Inside the circle are the outlines of large pieces of land, and the rest of the empty space is sea.

Question 4

Question 4. In the outline drawing beside this question, show the water and the land on the Earth by colouring them in different colours.

  • 1. Look at the outline. The pieces inside the lines are land.
  • 2. Colour the land green or brown.
  • 3. All the remaining space is water. Colour it blue.
  • 4. Below it, write or say: blue is water, green is land.

For teachers: Tell the children about the harm that wasting water does.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: If you cannot remember an answer, look back at the earlier pages: uses of water on page 35, natural sources on page 36, well and hand pump on page 37, and water shortage on page 39.
  • Vision: For Question 4, raise the lines of the outline with glue or string so that the land can be found by touch. Sandpaper or cloth can be stuck on the land and smooth paper on the water.
  • Movement: Instead of marking a tick ✓ or a cross ✗, say "true" or "false" or give a thumbs up or a thumbs down. For colouring use thick crayons, a sponge or finger paint; it does not matter if the colour goes outside the lines.
  • For teachers: Accept answers to Questions 2 and 3 orally, in signs or as drawings. In Question 3 each child's answer may differ according to their own area. Discuss the third sentence of Question 1: wells are made by people; the natural source is the underground water.

Answers

Question 1

  • 1. True
  • 2. False
  • 3. False
  • 4. True

Question 2

  • Sample answer: 1. We will have no water to drink and will stay thirsty. 2. We will not be able to make food. 3. We will not be able to have a bath or wash clothes and dishes.

Question 3

  • Sample answer: (i) Hand pump (ii) Canal. Other correct answers: well, tube well, river, spring, lake, rain, water from a filtration plant.

Question 4

  • Sample answer: The pieces of land are green or brown, and all the sea around them is blue.
