Uses of heat and light

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 73 of 96 (book page 69).

We use heat to keep ourselves warm, and we use light to light up our homes.

Picture: In a kitchen a woman is cooking food in a pot on the flame of a stove, and a man stands nearby holding a bag of vegetables.

Picture: A boy is walking with a lit torch in his hand, looking at the way in its light.

We need fire (heat) to cook food. We need light to see things.

Picture: A bright sun in a blue sky, and below it a field with many yellow sunflowers.

Caption: Sunlight is necessary for the growth of crops.

Try it

Which job needs heat and which job needs light?

Groups: Use of heat; Use of light.

Items:

  • Cooking food
  • Ironing clothes
  • Keeping ourselves warm
  • Lighting up the house
  • Reading a book at night
  • Seeing things in the dark

Activity

Divide the children into two groups. Each group should tour its school. One group should list the natural sources of heat and light while the other group lists the artificial sources, and present them in the classroom.

  • 1. Make two groups in the class: 'natural sources' and 'artificial sources'.
  • 2. Tour the school with your teacher: rooms, veranda, courtyard, kitchen.
  • 3. When you find something that gives light or heat, write it in your list or draw it.
  • 4. Come back to the classroom and present your list to everyone.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Remember two questions: 'Does it make things warm?' — then it is heat. 'Does it help us see?' — then it is light.
  • Vision: On the school tour look for heat instead of light: feel with your hand the sunny wall, the warm window sill and a cool shady spot (do not touch a bulb or stove).
  • Movement: On the tour each group should choose a route that a classmate using a wheelchair or walker can also follow. Instead of writing the list, say it or point to pictures.
  • For teachers: Explain 'natural' as 'made by Allah or already there' and 'artificial' as 'made by people'. In each group let different children be the writer, the finder and the presenter so everyone takes part according to their ability. Keep children away from electric switches and stoves.

Answers

Try it

  • Use of heat: Cooking food; Keeping ourselves warm; Ironing clothes
  • Use of light: Seeing things in the dark; Lighting up the house; Reading a book at night

Activity

  • Sample answer: Natural sources: the sun (sunshine); at night the moon and stars. Artificial (man-made) sources: bulb, tube light, torch, candle, heater, gas stove.
