Exercise: Animal homes

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 58 of 96 (book page 54).

Question 3

Question 3. Name two animals that live in the jungle. (i) ______ (ii) ______

  • 1. Look at the picture of the jungle on page 49.
  • 2. Write or say the names of two jungle animals.

Question 4

Look at the picture and say which animal's home it is.

  • b) Sparrow

3. Which animal lives in this place?

  • a) Sparrow
  • b) Camel
  • c) Rabbit

Question 5

Question 5. If you want to keep hens at home, what arrangements will you make for a place for them to live?

  • 1. Think: where will the hens sleep?
  • 2. Think: what will they eat and drink?
  • 3. Think: how will you keep them safe from cats, dogs, rain and cold?
  • 4. Write or say your answer in two or three sentences.

For teachers: Help the children to understand the importance of animals.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: For Question 4, look at page 52 again. Each animal is shown there with its home.
  • Vision: The three pictures are: an empty nest made of twigs, a burrow, which is a hole in the ground in the grass, and a desert with sand dunes. In the last picture, a white hen is with her chicks beside a wooden coop.
  • Movement: Instead of writing, say your answer aloud, or ask a grown-up to write it for you. You can also answer Question 5 by drawing a picture.
  • For teachers: For Question 5, let the children talk in pairs first. Listen to the experiences of children who keep hens at home. Accept every child's idea, even if it is one word or a gesture.

Answers

Question 3

  • Sample answer: (i) Lion (ii) Elephant. The monkey, giraffe and bear also live in the jungle.

Question 4

  • 1. c) Sparrow
  • 2. a) Rabbit
  • 3. b) Camel

Question 5

  • Sample answer: We will make a clean, airy coop of wood or wire mesh for the hens. We will put grain and clean water in it. At night we will close the coop so that the hens stay safe from cats and dogs.
