Where do animals live?

Animals live in different places.

Picture: An eagle with a white head sitting on a high rock, with the blue sky behind it.

Caption: The eagle lives in the mountains.

Picture: A rabbit sitting in front of its burrow, a hole in the ground.

Caption: The rabbit lives in a burrow.

Picture: A camel standing in a sandy desert.

Caption: The camel lives in the desert.

Picture: A lion sitting inside its den, a cave made of rocks.

Caption: The lion lives in a den.

Picture: A sparrow sitting in a nest made of twigs among the branches of a tree.

Caption: The sparrow lives in a nest.

Picture: Two dolphins leaping up out of the blue sea.

Caption: The dolphin lives in the sea.

Try it

Match each animal to its home.

First list:

  • 1. Eagle
  • 2. Rabbit
  • 3. Camel
  • 4. Lion
  • 5. Sparrow
  • 6. Dolphin

Second list:

  • Burrow
  • Den
  • Desert
  • Mountains
  • Nest
  • Sea

Think about it!

Tell us about some of the animals around you. Where are they found?

  • 1. Think of the names of two or three animals around you.
  • 2. For each animal, say where you have seen it.
  • 3. Listen to your partner as well.

Activity

Stick a picture of your favourite animal on a chart and give this information about it: picture, name, where it lives, what it eats, how many legs it has, and its tail.

  • 1. Stick or draw a picture of your favourite animal on a chart.
  • 2. Write its name.
  • 3. Write: where does it live? What does it eat?
  • 4. Write: how many legs does it have? Does it have a tail?

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look behind the animal in the picture. Is it sand, rocks, twigs or water? That is its home.
  • Understanding: Learn the new words slowly. A burrow is a rabbit's home, a hole in the ground. A den is a lion's cave. A nest is a bird's home made of twigs.
  • Movement: Ask a partner to help you make the chart: you say the answers, and your partner writes or sticks. You can also answer aloud.
  • For teachers: Bring real things for the six homes: sand, stones, twigs, a bowl of water. Children who are blind can touch them to recognise each animal's home. For the chart, accept every way of answering: writing, speaking or drawing.

Answers

Try it

  • 1. Eagle: Mountains
  • 2. Rabbit: Burrow
  • 3. Camel: Desert
  • 4. Lion: Den
  • 5. Sparrow: Nest
  • 6. Dolphin: Sea

Think about it!

  • Sample answer: A sparrow lives in a nest in a tree. Cats are found in houses and streets. Cows and buffaloes live in a cattle shed. Frogs are found near a pond.

Activity

  • Sample answer: Name: rabbit. Where does it live? In a burrow. What does it eat? Grass and carrots. How many legs does it have? Four. Tail: it has a short tail.
