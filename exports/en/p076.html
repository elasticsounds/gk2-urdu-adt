Chapter 12: Helping others

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Understand the importance of sharing things.
  • Name things that we use together with others.
  • Identify from the given stories and pictures how people help one another.
  • Recognise how people depend on one another in their daily lives.

Amna and Maryam study in the same class. One day Amna saw Maryam looking very worried. When Amna asked, Maryam told her that she had not understood her maths homework.

Amna said to Maryam, “Don't worry, I will explain it to you.” Amna explained all the homework to Maryam, and Maryam understood. In this way, with Amna's help, Maryam's worry went away.

Picture: Two girls, Amna and Maryam, sit at a table with an open notebook. Amna holds a pencil and explains the work to Maryam, who is smiling.

Try it

Read or listen to the story of Amna and Maryam. Then answer.

Put these in the right order:

  • Amna explained all the work to Maryam.
  • Amna saw Maryam looking very worried.
  • Maryam said she had not understood the maths homework.
  • Maryam's worry went away.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at the picture first: who is holding the pencil? That is Amna, explaining to Maryam. Then read the story.
  • Vision: Press the read-aloud button or ask a partner to read the story. In the picture two girls sit beside an open notebook.
  • Understanding: Remember the story in three parts: Maryam worried — Amna helped — Maryam happy.
  • For teachers: Have two children role-play Amna and Maryam. Children who cannot speak can join in with gestures or picture cards (worried, help, happy).

Answers
