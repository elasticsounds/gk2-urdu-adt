Exercise: Animals

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 57 of 96 (book page 53).

Exercise

Question 1

Choose the right word for the blank.

1. The elephant is a ___ animal.

  • a) air
  • b) mountains
  • c) water
  • d) land
  • e) den

2. The fish lives in ___.

  • a) air
  • b) mountains
  • c) water
  • d) land
  • e) den

3. The eagle lives in the ___.

  • a) air
  • b) mountains
  • c) water
  • d) land
  • e) den

4. The pigeon is a bird that flies in the ___.

  • a) air
  • b) mountains
  • c) water
  • d) land
  • e) den

5. The lion lives in a ___.

  • a) air
  • b) mountains
  • c) water
  • d) land
  • e) den

Question 2

Match each animal to its baby.

First list:

  • 1. Duck
  • 2. Elephant
  • 3. Lion
  • 4. Butterfly

Second list:

  • Baby elephant
  • Butterfly's baby
  • Ducklings
  • Lion cub

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In Question 1, first read the five words in the boxes. Cross out each word when you have used it. You will find the answers on pages 49 to 52.
  • Vision: In Question 2 on the printed page, on the right from top to bottom are: duck, elephant, lion, butterfly. On the left are: baby elephant, caterpillar, two ducklings, lion cub. You can say the pairs aloud.
  • Movement: If drawing a line is hard, put the same number or colour on both pictures, or point with your finger and let a partner draw the line.
  • For teachers: Read the sentences with blanks aloud and give out word cards, so that children can answer by placing a card instead of writing. Give extra time.

Answers

Question 1

  • 1. d) land
  • 2. c) water
  • 3. b) mountains
  • 4. a) air
  • 5. e) den

Question 2

  • 1. Duck: Ducklings
  • 2. Elephant: Baby elephant
  • 3. Lion: Lion cub
  • 4. Butterfly: Butterfly's baby
