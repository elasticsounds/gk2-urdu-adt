The National Anthem

Blessed be the sacred land; happy be the beautiful realm.

You are the symbol of high resolve, O land of Pakistan.

Blessed be the centre of faith.

The order of this sacred land is the strength of the brotherhood of the people.

May the nation, the country and the state shine in everlasting glory.

Blessed be the goal of our ambition.

The flag of the star and crescent leads the way to progress and perfection.

Interpreter of our past, glory of our present, inspiration of our future.

Shade of God, the Glorious and Mighty.

Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board, Lahore

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: When the national anthem is read or played we stand up straight with respect. Children who cannot stand can show respect by sitting up straight.
  • For teachers: Sing the anthem together. For deaf children, show the anthem in sign language or track the words with a finger; for children with low vision, teach it by listening and describe the picture of the Minar-e-Pakistan aloud.
