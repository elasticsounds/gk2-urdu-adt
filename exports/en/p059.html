Chapter 9: Agriculture and livestock

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Name the important crops grown in Pakistan.
  • Recognise that people make products from these crops.
  • Understand why keeping livestock is important.

Picture: A brown and white cow with long horns standing in a field of green grass.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In this chapter you will learn about two things: crops that grow in fields and livestock that people keep at home. Think: where did the roti you ate and the milk you drank today come from?
  • Understanding: Here are two new words. Crops are plants that grow in fields, like wheat. Livestock are animals that people keep, like cows, buffaloes and goats.
  • For teachers: Before you start the lesson, show real things: grains of wheat, cotton wool, jaggery, a carton of milk. Let the children touch them, smell them and name them. This is especially helpful for children who are blind and children who learn slowly.
