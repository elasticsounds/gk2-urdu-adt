Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at the cover pictures. Each one shows a good deed: planting a tree, helping, crossing the road safely. We will learn about all of these in this book.
  • Vision: Pause on each picture and listen to its description. Ask a buddy to tell you where each picture is.
  • For teachers: Ask the children: 'What good deed did you do today?' A child may answer by speaking, signing or pointing to a picture.
