The importance of forests

Picture: People are cutting down trees in a forest, and the cut logs lie on the ground. A man is walking along with a saw. In the middle the Earth, drawn as a globe, stands sick and sad with a thermometer in its mouth, holding a sign that says "SOS", which means "help".

Think and tell

Children! What are the people in the picture doing? Are they doing the right thing? If not, why?

  • 1. Say: what are the people in the picture doing?
  • 2. Say: are they doing the right thing?
  • 3. Say: if not, why?

Forests are the home of animals and birds. We get wood from forests, and furniture and sports goods are made from this wood.

Wood is also used as firewood. Matches and paper are made from wood. Forests also play an important part in keeping the weather pleasant.

Forests stop the soil from being washed away and protect us from floods. We should protect trees and not let them go to waste.

Picture: Three children are planting together: a boy is watering young trees with a watering can, and two girls are planting a new sapling in the soil with trowels.

Caption: Plant as many trees as possible to make up for the shortage of trees.

Try it

What do forests give us? Let's find out.

  • a) glass
  • b) paper

3. Forests stop the soil from being washed away and protect us from ___.

  • a) sunshine
  • b) wind
  • c) floods

4. Forests also play an important part in keeping the ___ pleasant.

  • a) road
  • b) weather
  • c) game

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look around you: the table, chair, door, pencil and notebook are all made from the wood of trees. Count how many things you can find!
  • Vision: Be sure to listen to the description of the first picture: the Earth is shown like a sick child asking for help. Touch a wooden object and paper to feel that both came from trees.
  • Understanding: Two pictures, two ideas. At the top, trees are being cut down and the Earth is sad. At the bottom, trees are being planted and the children are happy.
  • For teachers: Explain "SOS" in simple words as "help us". Let children answer by speaking, signing or holding up a happy or sad face card. If possible, plant a sapling at school and give every child a turn to water it.

Answers
