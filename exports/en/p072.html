Sources of light and heat

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 72 of 96 (book page 68).

There are some sources of light and heat that we use when we need them.

Picture: Pieces of wood are burning, with yellow flames and smoke rising.

Caption: By burning wood

Picture: A candle is burning in the dark and its flame is giving light.

Caption: Candle

Picture: The burner of a gas stove is lit, with blue flames coming out.

Caption: Gas

Picture: At night the electric street lamps along a road are on and the road is lit up.

Caption: Electricity

Do you know? In ancient times people discovered fire by rubbing stones together.

Uses of heat and light

Picture: In winter a family is sitting in a room. A fire is burning in the fireplace and a child is warming his hands near it. Two bulbs are lit on the ceiling. Father is reading a newspaper, Mother is knitting a sweater, and the children are wearing warm caps and scarves.

Activity

Rub both your hands together and observe what happens when you rub your hands for a long time.

  • 1. Put both palms together.
  • 2. Rub them quickly while counting to twenty.
  • 3. Now put your palms on your cheeks. What do you feel?
  • 4. Tell your partner what happened when you rubbed for a long time.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Four pictures, four sources: a wood fire, a candle, gas and electricity. Think: which of these are in your home?
  • For everyone: Never light or touch a fire, candle or stove on your own. Always have a grown-up with you.
  • Movement: If rubbing both hands is hard, rub one hand on your arm or leg, or let a partner rub their palm on yours. You will feel the heat just the same.
  • For teachers: The hand-rubbing activity suits blind and deaf children just as well, so do it with everyone and take each child's answer in their own way (by speaking, by signing or with a face card). Only you should show the lit candle, and keep the children at a distance.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: Rubbing your hands for a long time makes them warm, because rubbing makes heat.
