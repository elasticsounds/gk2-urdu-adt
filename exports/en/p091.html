Exercise: the benefits of forgiving

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 91 of 96 (book page 87).

Question 4

Question 4. Write the benefits of forgiving.

  • 1. Remember the story of Sara and Nida: what happened after forgiving?
  • 2. Write one benefit in each box.
  • 3. If writing is hard, say it aloud or draw a picture.

For teachers: Tell the children moral stories or incidents that teach a lesson.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: To think of a benefit, ask yourself: how does my heart feel after forgiving? How does my friend's heart feel? What happens to our friendship?
  • Vision: The page has four empty boxes, two above and two below. You can record your four benefits by speaking, or dictate them to a buddy.
  • Movement: If writing in small boxes is hard, draw four big boxes on a large sheet, or put one picture or sticker in each box.
  • For teachers: First gather benefits orally from the whole class and write them on the board with pictures (friendship, happiness, peace, love). Then let each child write, draw, or choose words from the board, as suits them. When telling stories, use pictures and gestures alongside.

Answers

Question 4

  • Sample answer: 1. We become friends. 2. The heart becomes happy and light. 3. Fights and quarrels end. 4. Everyone lives together with love.
