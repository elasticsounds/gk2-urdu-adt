Air

Air helps us to breathe. It also helps plants to make food, and it helps birds and aeroplanes to fly.

Try it

Do we get this thing from the land or from water? Put the picture in the right box.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at the picture first, then read the sentence under it. Ask yourself: what did I use today that comes from the land, from water or from the air? (Bread, fish, a glass of water, a breath.)
  • Vision: Get to know things by touching them: a clay bowl or flowerpot (land), a dish full of water (water), and the air from a fan or from blowing (air).
  • Hearing: Agree on three signs. For land, pat downwards with your palm. For water, make waves with your fingers. For air, wave your hand in front of your mouth. Make the right sign for each picture.
  • For teachers: Make picture cards and ask the children to sort them into three piles (land, water, air). A child who cannot speak can answer by picking up a card or by pointing. Add local examples (a potter, a canal, a kite).
