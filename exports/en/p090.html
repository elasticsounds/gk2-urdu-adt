Forgiveness: key points and exercise

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 90 of 96 (book page 86).

Key points:

  • We should not hurt anyone's feelings.
  • If we hurt someone's feelings by mistake, we should say sorry.
  • If someone hurts our feelings, it is better that we forgive them.
  • We should not make fun of anyone.
  • We should treat others with good manners.

For teachers: Tell a story of forgiveness from the blessed life of Hazrat Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah and the Last of the Prophets (peace and blessings be upon him, his family and his companions).

Exercise

Question 1

Question 1. Did Nida treat Sara well in class on the first day?

  • 1. First write "yes" or "no".
  • 2. Then write what Nida did.

Question 2

Question 2. If you were in Sara's place, what would you do?

  • 1. Think about what Sara did.
  • 2. Write what you would do.

Question 3

Question 3. Have you ever forgiven someone for a mistake? How did you feel after forgiving?

  • 1. Write: whom did you forgive?
  • 2. Write: what mistake had they made?
  • 3. Write: how did you feel after forgiving?

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Count the five key points on your fingers: do not hurt, say sorry, forgive, do not make fun, be polite.
  • Movement: If writing is hard, say your answer aloud and let a buddy or adult write it down. On screen the "easy" level needs only one tap.
  • Understanding: For Question 1, look again at the picture on the previous page: Nida is laughing. Is that good behaviour?
  • For teachers: Questions 2 and 3 have many right answers; accept every kind answer. Answers may be oral, signed, drawn or a single word. Provide happy, sad and angry face cards so a child can hold one up to show a feeling.

Answers

Question 1

  • Sample answer: No. Nida did not treat Sara well on the first day. She kept calling her bad names and laughing at her.

Question 2

  • Sample answer: If I were in Sara's place, I too would help Nida and forgive her.

Question 3

  • Sample answer: Yes. My sister spilled water on my notebook. She said sorry and I forgave her. After forgiving I felt happy.
