Water is a sign of life

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 38 of 96 (book page 34).

Dear children! Have you ever wondered: if there were no water, would there be any living things on Earth?

Picture: Clear blue water is splashing up and its drops are flying in the air.

Water is a sign of life

Water is very important for all living things. Living things cannot stay alive without water.

Picture: A boy is drinking water from a glass.

Picture: Water is being poured from a watering can onto two small plants growing in soil.

Think about it!

Why is water necessary for living things?

  • 1. Look at both pictures: the boy is drinking water and the plants are being watered.
  • 2. Think: how would you feel if you got no water all day?
  • 3. Tell your partner or teacher your answer.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: A way to remember: anything that breathes and grows (people, animals, plants) needs water. Find three such things in your home.
  • Vision: Touch two flowerpots: one with wet soil and one with dry soil. The plant in wet soil has soft, fresh leaves.
  • Hearing: Use the signs for 'water', 'drink' and 'plant'. The 'Think about it' answer can also be given in signs or by drawing.
  • For teachers: Make pairs for 'Think about it'. Children who cannot speak or write can answer by pointing at a picture or holding up a picture card. Give every child a turn to water a class plant each day.

Answers

Think about it!

  • Sample answer: Water is very important for all living things. Living things cannot stay alive without water. People and animals drink water, and plants grow with water.
