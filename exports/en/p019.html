Exercise: Villages and cities (Questions 1–3)

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 19 of 96 (book page 15).

Exercise

Question 1

Who will you go to? Match each task with the right person.

First list:

  • 1. To buy meat
  • 2. To have a shoe mended
  • 3. To have clothes sewn
  • 4. To get treatment

Second list:

  • Butcher
  • Cobbler
  • Doctor
  • Tailor

Question 2

Put each thing in the right box: does it belong to the city or the village?

Groups: City; Village.

Items:

  • Airport
  • Big roads
  • Farmer
  • Noise
  • Pure air
  • Small mud houses
  • Smoke from vehicles
  • Springs and wells

Question 3

Which occupation would you like to choose when you grow up, and why?

  • 1. Write the occupation you like best.
  • 2. Write why you want to do this work.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In Question 1, read the task first, then think: "Who does this work?" For Question 2, look again at the pictures on pages 12 and 13.
  • Movement: If drawing lines is hard, say the answer aloud, touch both boxes with a finger, or touch the answers on the screen to match them. The answer to Question 3 can also be spoken and recorded.
  • Vision: Listen to the eight words in Question 2 one at a time, and say "city" or "village" after each one.
  • For teachers: In Question 3 no answer is wrong. Encourage every child's dream, and accept a drawing, a gesture or a spoken answer instead of writing. The butcher is not shown in the book's pictures, so explain first that a butcher sells meat.

Answers

Question 1

  • 1. To buy meat: Butcher
  • 2. To have a shoe mended: Cobbler
  • 3. To have clothes sewn: Tailor
  • 4. To get treatment: Doctor

Question 2

  • City: Airport; Smoke from vehicles; Noise; Big roads
  • Village: Pure air; Farmer; Springs and wells; Small mud houses

Question 3

  • Sample answer: When I grow up I want to be a doctor because I want to treat sick people.
