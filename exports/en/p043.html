Shortage of water

The reasons for the shortage of water are as follows:

  • 1. Not enough underground water
  • 2. Not storing rainwater
  • 3. Poor arrangements for supplying water
  • 4. Using more water than needed

Because there is not enough rain, some parts of Pakistan, such as Tharparkar, Badin, Dadu, Chagai and other places, are always short of water.

In the desert there is a shortage of water, and people have to fetch water from far away. Because there is so little water, people face many problems.

Note: We should use water carefully and should not waste it.

Picture: In a desert, four women in brightly coloured clothes walk across the sand carrying water pots on their heads.

Caption: Desert

Activity

With your parents' help, find out which areas are short of water. The table has two columns: Area and Reason.

  • 1. Ask your mother, father or another adult: which areas are short of water?
  • 2. Also ask: why is there so little water there?
  • 3. Write the names of three areas in the "Area" column.
  • 4. Next to each area, write the reason in the "Reason" column.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look closely at the picture: the women are carrying heavy water pots on their heads from far away. Think: if you had to walk that far for every glass of water, would you waste any?
  • Vision: Carry a bottle or small bucket full of water once around the classroom and feel how heavy water is. Touch dry sand to understand the desert.
  • Movement: If writing in the table is hard, say your answers aloud, and your mother, father or a partner can write down your words. The answer can also be recorded as sound.
  • For teachers: Show Tharparkar, Badin, Dadu and Chagai on a map of Pakistan. Send parents a one-line note about the home task. For children who cannot get help at home, do the same task in pairs in class the next day so that no child is left out.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: Area: Tharparkar. Reason: there is very little rain and it is a desert. Area: Chagai. Reason: there is no rain and little underground water. Area: Badin. Reason: there is little rain and the arrangements for supplying water are poor.
