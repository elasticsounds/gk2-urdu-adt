Exercise: Agriculture and livestock

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 64 of 96 (book page 60).

Exercise

Question 1

Look at the picture and say which crop the thing is made from.

1. Which crop are biscuits made from?

  • a) Cotton
  • b) Wheat
  • c) Sugarcane

2. Which crop is the sweater made from?

  • a) Cotton
  • b) Sugarcane
  • c) Wheat

3. Which crop is brown sugar made from?

  • a) Wheat
  • b) Cotton
  • c) Sugarcane

Question 2

Question 2. Write the names of any three things obtained from livestock. (i) ______ (ii) ______ (iii) ______

  • 1. Look again at the pictures on pages 58 and 59.
  • 2. Write or say the names of three things we get from livestock.

Question 3

Question 3. Write the names of any two important crops of Pakistan. (i) ______ (ii) ______

  • 1. Read the first lines of page 56 again.
  • 2. Write or say the names of two important crops.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: For Question 1, think backwards. Biscuits come from flour, and flour comes from which crop? A sweater comes from thread, thread comes from cotton wool, and cotton wool comes from which crop? Brown sugar comes from juice, and the juice comes from which crop? The answers are on pages 56 to 58.
  • Vision: The three pictures are: biscuits on a plate, a grey sweater, and brown sugar in a bowl. You can touch a real biscuit, a sweater and some brown sugar, and answer aloud.
  • Movement: If writing on the line is hard, place a card with the crop's name under the picture or say it aloud.
  • For teachers: In fact, a sweater can also be made from wool, which comes from sheep. By the book's lesson the expected answer is "cotton"; if a child says "wool", praise them and explain the difference.

Answers

Question 1

  • 1. b) Wheat
  • 2. a) Cotton
  • 3. c) Sugarcane

Question 2

  • Sample answer: (i) Milk (ii) Meat (iii) Skin (leather)

Question 3

  • Sample answer: (i) Wheat (ii) Cotton. Rice, sugarcane and maize are also correct answers.
