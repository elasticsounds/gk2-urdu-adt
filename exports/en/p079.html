Exercise: Helping others

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 79 of 96 (book page 75).

Question 2

If your friend did not come to school because of illness, how will you help him? (i) (ii)

  • 1. Think: what will a sick friend need?
  • 2. Write two ways to help: (i) and (ii).

Question 3

If a flood comes to an area, how can you help the people? (i) (ii)

  • 1. Think: what things do people need in a flood?
  • 2. Write two ways to help: (i) and (ii).

For teachers: Tell the children stories of helping others from the blessed life of Hazrat Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah and the Last of the Prophets (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, his family and his companions).

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: To think of an answer, ask yourself: if I were ill, or water came into my home, what would I need?
  • Movement: Instead of writing, you can record your answer by speaking, or a partner can write down the sentences you say.
  • Understanding: Do one question at a time. First pick the helpful things from picture cards (food, water, clothes, medicine), then make a sentence.
  • For teachers: Talking about floods may bring back painful memories for some children; speak gently and keep the focus on “what we can do”. Accept written, spoken, drawn and signed answers alike.

Answers

Question 2

  • Sample answer: (i) I will ask how he is and pray for him. (ii) I will tell him about the school work and explain it to him.

Question 3

  • Sample answer: (i) We can give them food, clean water and clothes. (ii) Together with grown-ups we can collect relief goods and money for them.
