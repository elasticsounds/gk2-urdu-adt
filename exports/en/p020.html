Exercise: Villages and cities (Questions 4 and 5)

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 20 of 96 (book page 16).

Question 4

Write any three facilities that you have in a city but not in a village.

  • 1. Look again at the "City life" pictures on pages 12 and 13.
  • 2. Think: which of these things are not found in a village?
  • 3. Write three such facilities.

Question 5

Which facility would you like to provide first in a village, and why?

  • 1. Remember: villages have fewer health and education facilities.
  • 2. Choose one facility that you would like to give first.
  • 3. Write the reason.

For teachers: Explain to the children in detail how city life and village life are different (way of living and facilities).

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: A "facility" is something that makes our life easier, like a school, a hospital, a road, electricity or clean water. To find the answers, look at the pictures in the chapter again.
  • Movement: If writing is hard, say the answers and let a partner or the teacher write them; or choose picture cards of facilities and place them on the lines.
  • Understanding: For Question 5, just choose one of four: school, hospital, clean water or road.
  • For teachers: Question 5 is an opinion question; accept every answer for which the child gives a reason. Do not show villages as less good: remind the children of the good things about villages too (pure air, quiet, open space).

Answers

Question 4

  • Sample answer: 1. University 2. Airport 3. Big roads. "Big hospitals" is also a correct answer, because cities have the best health and education facilities.

Question 5

  • Sample answer: I would like to build a hospital in the village first, so that sick people can be treated in the village itself and do not have to go far away to the city.
