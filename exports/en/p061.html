Activity

Bring seeds of different crops to the classroom and tell your classmates which crop those seeds are from.

  • 1. Bring seeds of two or three crops from home, such as wheat, rice, maize or a pulse.
  • 2. Put each seed on a separate paper or in a bottle cap.
  • 3. Show them to your classmates and tell them which crop each seed comes from.
  • 4. Look at other children's seeds and try to recognise them.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Remember cotton's journey: cotton, then cotton wool, then thread, then cloth. Touch your shirt: it is made of threads too.
  • Vision: Touch a ball of cotton wool, a reel of thread and a piece of cloth in turn. Recognise seeds by shape and size: wheat is long, maize is big and flat, rice is thin.
  • Movement: If picking up small seeds is hard, keep them in bowls or bottle caps, then just point and say the names.
  • For teachers: Seeds are small; make sure no child puts them in the mouth, nose or ear. Share your own seeds with children who could not bring any so that everyone is included.
