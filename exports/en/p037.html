Chapter 6: Water

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Know that water is needed to stay alive and that water is very important for living things.
  • Identify the natural sources of water.
  • Identify the main sources of water in your surroundings and describe their importance.
  • Explain how water reaches homes from natural sources.
  • Know that clean water is used for drinking and cooking.
  • Understand that filtering and boiling water are ways of cleaning water.
  • Know that some areas of Pakistan are short of water.

Picture: A drop has fallen into blue water and round ripples are spreading across the surface.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Before you start, think: where have you used water since this morning? For drinking, for washing your face, for washing before prayers? This chapter is about just these things.
  • Vision: Take a bowl of water and touch it with a finger. Ripples form on the water, just like in the picture. Feel the ripples with your hand.
  • Understanding: Do not try to remember all seven points at once. Just remember three things: we need water, water should be clean, and we save water.
  • For teachers: Start the chapter by showing a glass of water, a jug and a tap in class. Let every child touch, see or hear the water. Teach the signs for 'water' and 'drink' to all children so that deaf children are included too.
