Exercise: Name the profession in the picture

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 83 of 96 (book page 79).

Question 4

Look at each picture. What work does this person do? Name the profession.

First list:

  • 1. Picture 1
  • 2. Picture 2
  • 3. Picture 3
  • 4. Picture 4
  • 5. Picture 5

Second list:

  • Cobbler
  • Doctor
  • Driver
  • Sweet-maker
  • Teacher

For teachers: Tell the children that every profession is worthy of respect and that the people in each profession help us in one way or another.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In each picture look for the clue that tells the profession: steering wheel, sweet shop, board, white coat, shoes.
  • Vision: Listen to the picture descriptions: (1) an elderly man holds a bus steering wheel, (2) a man stands in a sweet shop holding a tray of sweets, (3) a lady teacher points to numbers on a board, (4) a lady in a white coat and mask, (5) an elderly man is stitching shoes.
  • Movement: Instead of writing the profession, you can say it, place a name card next to the picture, or match on the screen.
  • For teachers: For the fourth picture accept both “doctor” and “nurse”. At the end have every child say “Every profession deserves respect” — and thank the school guard, cleaner and driver.
