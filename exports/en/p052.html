Exercise: Plants

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 52 of 96 (book page 48).

Exercise

Question 1

Which vegetable is the root of a plant and which is not? Put each vegetable in the right box.

Groups: It is a root ✓; It is not a root.

Items:

  • Carrot
  • Ginger
  • Mint
  • Radish
  • Tomato
  • Turnip

Question 2

Look at the picture. Choose the name of the plant part and its job.

1. What is the name of this part?

  • a) Root
  • b) Leaf
  • c) Stem

2. What is the job of a leaf?

  • a) It makes the plant's food with the help of water, air and light
  • b) It holds the plant in the soil
  • c) It makes fruit and seeds

3. What is the name of this part?

  • a) Flower
  • b) Stem
  • c) Root

4. What is the job of a root?

  • a) It makes the plant more beautiful
  • b) It holds the plant firmly in the soil and soaks up water
  • c) It makes the plant's food

Question 3

What will happen if there are no trees and plants around us? Name any three harms.

  • 1. Think: what do plants give us? (oxygen, fruit, wood, clean air)
  • 2. Now think: what will happen if we do not get these things? Write or say three harms.

Question 4

What benefits do we get from plants? Write any two benefits. (i) ______ (ii) ______

  • 1. Look at the chapter pages again: what do plants give us?
  • 2. Write or say any two benefits.

Question 5

Name three plants that flowers grow on. (i) ______ (ii) ______ (iii) ______

  • 1. Think: on which plants have you seen flowers at home, at school or in a garden?
  • 2. Write or say the names of three such plants.

For teachers: Help the children to understand the importance of plants.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: If you forget an answer, look at the chapter pages again: parts of a plant (page 42), roots that we eat (page 44), flowering plants (page 45), benefits of plants (pages 46 and 47).
  • Movement: Instead of writing, you can say the answer aloud. In Question 1 you can point to the right vegetable and a partner can put the tick ✓ for you.
  • Vision: For Question 2, hold a real leaf and a small plant with its root. Feel them and say which part is the leaf and which is the root.
  • For teachers: Do Questions 3 and 4 aloud with the class first, and write the children's ideas on the board with pictures. Then let each child write, draw or say the answer, whichever suits them. Accept every correct idea, not only the book's words.

Answers

Question 1

  • It is a root ✓: Carrot; Radish; Ginger; Turnip
  • It is not a root: Tomato; Mint

Question 2

  • 1. b) Leaf
  • 2. a) It makes the plant's food with the help of water, air and light
  • 3. c) Root
  • 4. b) It holds the plant firmly in the soil and soaks up water

Question 3

  • Sample answer: 1. We will not get oxygen to breathe. 2. We will not get fruit to eat or wood for furniture. 3. The air will not stay clean and the Earth's temperature will rise.

Question 4

  • Sample answer: (i) Plants give us oxygen to breathe and fruit to eat. (ii) Houses and furniture are made from the wood of trees.

Question 5

  • Sample answer: (i) Rose (ii) Sunflower (iii) Jasmine
