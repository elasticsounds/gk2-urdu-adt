Chapter 1: Our homeland Pakistan

Picture: The tall pointed minarets and tent-shaped roof of the Faisal Mosque in Islamabad, against a cloudy blue sky.

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • recognise the map of Pakistan.
  • name the provinces and federal areas of Pakistan.
  • show the culture of the four provinces and the federal areas of Pakistan.
  • say why the national flag is important.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: In this chapter we learn four things about our country Pakistan: the map, the provinces, the culture (language, dress, food) and the flag. Think: which province or area do you live in?
  • Vision: The background picture shows the Faisal Mosque in Islamabad. Its four tall minarets are pointed like pencils. Listen to the picture description.
  • For teachers: Before starting the chapter, show a real flag and a map in class and let the children touch them. Ask each child the name of their town or village; a child may say it, sign it or write it.
