Animals and their babies

The babies of some animals look just like them from the day they are born.

Activity

Bring pictures of different animals and their babies to class and help the pupils to recognise the baby animals.

  • 1. Bring a picture of an animal and its baby from home, or from an old newspaper or magazine, or draw one yourself.
  • 2. Show your picture to the class. Say the name of the animal and the name of its baby.
  • 3. Say: does the baby look like its parents, or does it look different?
  • 4. Stick all the pictures on one chart.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: First look at the big animal, then at its baby. Ask yourself: do they look alike?
  • Vision: A frog's baby has no legs and has a long tail. A butterfly's baby is a caterpillar, which has no wings. Make both shapes from clay or dough and feel the difference with your hands.
  • Understanding: Look at only one pair at a time. Say: "cat, kitten", "hen, chick". Make the animal's sound as well.
  • For teachers: Give class picture cards to children who cannot bring a picture, or let them draw one. Children can take part by speaking, by using gestures or by matching cards.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: This is a cat and this is its baby, a kitten. The kitten looks like the cat.
