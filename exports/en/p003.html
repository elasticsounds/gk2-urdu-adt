Helpful hints

  • For everyone: This page is for grown-ups. It lists the people who wrote the book, drew the pictures and checked it. You can go on to the next page.
  • For teachers: Tell the children that many people work together to make a book: the author writes, the illustrator draws. Ask: 'Which job would you like to do in making a book?'
