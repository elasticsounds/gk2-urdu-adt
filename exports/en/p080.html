Chapter 13: Professions

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Identify some professions (teaching, farming, medicine, etc.) from pictures.
  • Understand the role of some professions in your daily life.
  • Say which profession you like most and why.

In daily life, we have to take up some profession to earn a living, such as a tailor to sew clothes, a greengrocer to sell vegetables, a doctor to treat patients, and so on. In this way we not only earn a living but also help others.

Picture: A lady doctor in a white coat is checking the throat of a little girl who sits on a stool with her mouth open. A wheelchair stands nearby.

Try it

Who does which job? Match them.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at the picture: who is the lady in the white coat? Then think about the workers in your own neighbourhood.
  • Vision: Listen to the picture description. Recognise professions by touching their tools: a tailor's tape and thread, a doctor's stethoscope, a greengrocer's vegetables.
  • Hearing: Agree on a gesture for each profession (sewing, weighing, checking a pulse) and show the gesture along with the name.
  • For teachers: The word “profession” is new: explain it as “work done to earn a living”. Use the wheelchair in the picture to talk about how people with disabilities can take up any profession too.
