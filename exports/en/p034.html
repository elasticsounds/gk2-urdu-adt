Minerals

Minerals include iron, coal, salt and precious stones. We make many different useful things from them.

Activity

Write the uses of things made from the materials given below. Material and its uses Clay: 1, 2, 3 Wood: 1, 2, 3 Iron: 1, 2, 3

  • 1. First think about clay: what things are made from clay? Write three things.
  • 2. Then write three things made from wood.
  • 3. Last of all, write three things made from iron.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look around you and ask each thing: “What are you made of?” The desk is made of wood, the window grille of iron and the flowerpot of clay. This will make the table easy to fill in.
  • Vision: Get to know the three materials by touching them: iron is cold, hard and heavy; wood is light and a little warm; a clay pot is rough. Tap them gently and listen to the different sounds as well.
  • Movement: Instead of writing, the child can point to things in the room, say the answers, or put real things into three baskets (clay, wood, iron).
  • For teachers: Bring real things made of all three materials to class (a clay bowl, a wooden ruler, an iron key). In the table, accept one answer for each material instead of nine answers, and accept answers given as drawings too.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: Clay: 1. dishes (water pot, bowl) 2. bricks and houses 3. flowerpots. Wood: 1. table and chair 2. doors and windows 3. pencil and paper. Iron: 1. hand pump 2. nail and hammer 3. lock and key.
