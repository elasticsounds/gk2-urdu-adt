How a plant grows

Project

Project: Take two pots that have plants growing in them. Put one pot outside in the courtyard where it keeps getting light, water and air, while you keep the other plant inside a room where it gets no light. Observe both plants every three days and write the results in the given table. The table has columns for observation after 3 days, after 6 days and after 9 days, and for the result. It has one row for pot number 1, kept in the courtyard, and one row for pot number 2, kept in the room.

  • 1. Take two pots with the same kind of plant in them. Write 1 on one pot and 2 on the other.
  • 2. Put pot number 1 in the courtyard where it gets light, water and air.
  • 3. Put pot number 2 inside a room where it gets no light.
  • 4. After 3, 6 and 9 days look at both plants: what colour are the leaves? Is the plant straight or drooping?
  • 5. Write what you saw in the table, or draw it. At the end write the result.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Make the shapes with your hands: for broad, spread both hands wide; for oval, make an egg with your hands; for small, bring two fingers close together; for long and narrow, move your hands far apart.
  • Vision: Touch real leaves to find out their shape. Run a finger round the edge of the leaf: is it broad, egg-like, small, or long and thin?
  • Movement: If writing in the table is hard, have a photo of the plants taken every third day, or say your observation aloud and let a partner write it.
  • For teachers: Do the project in pairs: one child waters the plant, and the other looks and says what they see. Instead of writing in the table, let children draw a smiling face if the plant is fine or a sad face if the plant has wilted. After the experiment put the indoor plant in the sun too so that it recovers.

Answers

Project

  • Sample answer: Pot number 1, in the courtyard: after 3, 6 and 9 days the plant stayed green and upright and kept growing. Pot number 2, in the room: the leaves slowly turned yellow and the plant became weak and drooped. Result: a plant needs light, water and air to grow.
