Stopping forests from being cut down

Picture: On a hillside many trees have been cut down and their trunks lie scattered on the ground; a little forest is left behind them.

Caption: Wood should not be cut without a reason.

Picture: A pot sits on a small gas stove; food is cooking without any wood being burnt.

Caption: Use other sources of fuel instead of wood.

Picture: A forest is on fire at night; yellow and orange flames and smoke rise among the trees.

Caption: Save forests from catching fire.

Try it

Each picture tells us one way to save forests. Choose the correct answer.

Activity

Make a poster in a group to tell how we can keep natural resources safe.

  • 1. Make a group of three or four children.
  • 2. Think together: how can we save water and forests?
  • 3. Draw or paste pictures on chart paper, such as a tap turned off, a new sapling or a green forest.
  • 4. Write a slogan in big letters at the top.
  • 5. Show your poster to the class and explain how resources are kept safe.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Three pictures show three ways to save forests: cut fewer trees, burn another fuel instead of wood, and protect forests from fire. Count them on your fingers to remember.
  • Vision: The fire picture is very bright; if it hurts your eyes, listen to its description instead. Stick raised things on the poster, such as real leaves, twigs, cotton wool and thread, so that it can be read by touch.
  • Movement: If holding a pen or scissors is hard, you can give the group ideas and the slogan, choose the pictures, or present the poster to the class. That work counts just as much.
  • For teachers: Make mixed groups and give each child a role that fits their strengths (artist, writer, paster, presenter). For deaf children write the steps on the board or show them with signs. Reassure any child who is frightened by the fire picture.

Answers

Try it

  • 1. b) Wood should not be cut without a reason.
  • 2. a) Use other sources of fuel instead of wood.
  • 3. c) Save forests from catching fire.

Activity

  • Sample answer: Slogan: "Save water, plant trees!" Ways: keep the tap turned off, use water carefully, do not cut wood without a reason, save forests from fire and plant as many trees as possible.
