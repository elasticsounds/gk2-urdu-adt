The role of plants in climate change

The Earth is getting hotter because of the things people do. Plants help to bring its temperature down. They keep the air clean, make the weather pleasant and make the environment beautiful.

For this, we should do these things:

  • We should plant as many trees as we can.
  • We should save forests from catching fire.
  • We should take care of trees and plants.
  • We should not cut down trees without a reason.

Picture: A picture of the Earth as a globe. On the right, black smoke rises from factories and cars and spreads over the Earth, and red arrows show its path. On the left are a green tree, plants and a man. Plants help to stop this smoke.

Activity

With your teacher's help, hold a tree-planting week at school. Children should plant saplings, water the saplings they have planted regularly, and keep them safe.

  • 1. Together with your teacher, choose a place at school to plant a sapling.
  • 2. Dig a hole, put the sapling in and press the soil down around it.
  • 3. Water the plant and put a label with your name beside it.
  • 4. Water your plant every day for the whole week and keep it safe.

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at the picture in two halves. On the right there is smoke from cars and factories. On the left there is a green tree. Trees clean the air.
  • For everyone: On a hot day, stand in the shade of a tree and then in the sun. Feel the difference: it is cooler under the tree.
  • Movement: If digging the hole is hard, you can put the seed or sapling in the hole, or water it with a small watering can. You can also plant it in a pot on a table.
  • For teachers: During tree planting, give every child a job: one chooses the place, one adds the soil, one waters, and one makes the name label. Put pots on a raised surface for children who use wheelchairs. Make a picture chart of watering turns so that every child can find their day.
