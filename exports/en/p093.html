The jungle court: the monkey's complaint

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 93 of 96 (book page 89).

All the animals of the jungle were gathered under the banyan tree. The fox ordered everyone to be silent. Here the lion, king of the jungle, was to give his decision on the quarrel between the monkey and the elephant.

Picture: Animals are gathered under big trees in a jungle. The lion stands on the right, with the fox in front of him. On the left are an angry-looking elephant and a giraffe. Behind are a deer, a peacock, ducks and a bear. A small monkey sits on a tree branch.

First of all, the fox asked the monkey to tell his side of the story. The monkey greeted the lion and said: "Your Majesty! I have been wronged in your kingdom. This elephant has been troubling me for many days. He breaks the branches of the very tree I swing on. I had collected fruit for my children. Yesterday he stole all the fruit. Please give me justice."

Activity

In daily life, have you ever felt that you were treated unfairly at home or at school? (Describe an incident.)

  • 1. Say: what happened, and where?
  • 2. Say: how did you feel?
  • 3. Think: what would have been fair? What should have been done?

Try it

Remember the story and say who is who in the jungle court.

1. Where were the jungle animals gathered?

  • a) On the river bank
  • b) Under the banyan tree
  • c) On top of the mountain

2. Who was the quarrel between?

  • a) The monkey and the elephant
  • b) The lion and the fox
  • c) The bear and the pigeon

3. Who ordered everyone to be silent?

  • a) The elephant
  • b) The monkey
  • c) The fox

4. What did the monkey ask the lion for?

  • a) Fruit
  • b) Justice
  • c) A new tree

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: This is a jungle court. The lion is the judge and the monkey is complaining. Think: should the lion also listen to the elephant before deciding?
  • Vision: In the picture the big lion is on the right, the orange fox in the middle and the grey elephant on the left. The monkey is very small on a branch at the top right. Retell the story by touching toy animals.
  • Hearing: Make one sign for each animal (lion: claws, elephant: arm as a trunk, monkey: scratching under the arm) and tell the story with the signs.
  • For teachers: Some children may become emotional when describing an unfair incident; listen calmly and do not blame any child or family member. Children who cannot speak may draw, show a card or share through a buddy. When role-playing the story, give every child a part.

Answers

Activity

  • Sample answer: One day everyone got a turn in the game but I did not. I felt very sad. It would have been fair to let everyone play in turn.
