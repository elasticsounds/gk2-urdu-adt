Exercise: Our homeland Pakistan

General Knowledge, Class 2. Page 14 of 96 (book page 10).

Question 1 (continued)

Choose the correct answer to each question.

1. How many provinces does Pakistan have?

  • a) Four
  • b) Five
  • c) Six

2. How many colours are there in Pakistan's flag?

  • a) Two
  • b) Three
  • c) Four

For teachers: Tell the children about the four provinces of Pakistan and the areas administered by the federal government.

Question 2

Read or listen to each statement. If it is right, choose "True". If it is not, choose "False".

  • 1. In Pakistan's flag, the green colour stands for other religions. (True / False)
  • 2. The capital of Azad Jammu and Kashmir is Muzaffarabad. (True / False)
  • 3. By area, Balochistan is the smallest province of Pakistan. (True / False)
  • 4. By population, Punjab is the largest province of Pakistan. (True / False)
  • 5. Gilgit-Baltistan is in the south of Pakistan. (True / False)

Question 3

What is the name of the capital of Pakistan?

  • 1. Read the question: What is the name of the capital of Pakistan?
  • 2. Write the answer in your notebook, or say it aloud.

Question 4

Put each thing in the box of its province: Sindh or Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?

Groups: Sindh; Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Items:

  • Dress: Chitrali cap, karakul cap and Peshawari sandals
  • Dress: ajrak and Sindhi cap
  • Food: pulao and chapli kabab
  • Food: rice, koki and spinach
  • Language: Pashto, Hindko, Khowar
  • Language: Sindhi

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: If you cannot remember an answer, go back and find it in the book: the flag is on page 3, Sindh on page 4, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa on pages 5 and 6. Looking back is a good habit.
  • Movement: If writing in the boxes is hard, say the answers and let a partner write them, or drag each answer into the right box on the screen. Instead of a tick and a cross, you can also show thumbs up or thumbs down.
  • Understanding: Do only one question at a time. In the true or false question, first listen to the sentence, then think: is this what the book said?
  • For teachers: For Question 4, let the children sort real objects or picture cards (ajrak, cap, rice, kabab) into two baskets, "Sindh" and "Khyber Pakhtunkhwa". Accept every answer, whether it is written, spoken, shown by gesture or given by placing a picture, and give extra time.
