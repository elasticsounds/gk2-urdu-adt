Try it

Put each thing in the right box: is it made from sugarcane juice or from milk?

Groups: From sugarcane juice; From milk.

Items:

  • Butter
  • Cheese
  • Ghee
  • Jaggery
  • Sugar
  • Yoghurt

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Look at home today: tea has both sugar and milk. The sugar came from sugarcane and the milk from a cow or buffalo.
  • Vision: Recognise jaggery and sugar by taste and touch: jaggery is a soft, sticky lump and sugar is fine grains. Recognise yoghurt and butter by smell and taste too.
  • Understanding: Make two groups and place one thing at a time: "sweet things come from sugarcane" and "milk things come from the cow". Repeat the full sentence each time.
  • For teachers: If possible bring a little jaggery, sugar, yoghurt and butter to class. Ask parents about allergies first. Children can answer by tasting, pointing or choosing a picture.
