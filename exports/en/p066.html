Chapter 10: Protecting the Earth's resources

Learning outcomes

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

  • Identify how people waste water.
  • Know what problems arise when water is wasted.
  • Describe ways to stop water being wasted.
  • Understand why people need forests.
  • Identify how human activities (cutting down forests) are harming the earth.
  • Suggest ways in which the cutting down of forests can be stopped.

The earth's resources are very important for human life. Life is impossible without water.

Water is our everyday need, so we should use it carefully. When we waste water we have to face many problems.

Try it

Read or listen to the text above, then answer.

  • 1. The earth's resources are very important for human life. (True / False)
  • 2. Life is possible even without water. (True / False)
  • 3. Water should be used carefully. (True / False)
  • 4. Wasting water causes no problem. (True / False)

Helpful hints

  • For everyone: Before reading, think: if no water came to your home for one day, which jobs would stop?
  • Vision: Behind the text there is a green tree inside a glass globe; it is only decoration. The real content is in the list and the last paragraph, so listen to them.
  • Understanding: Do not try to remember all six points. Just remember two words: water and forest. We must save both.
  • For teachers: Start the chapter with a glass of water and a leaf or twig in front of the class, so every child can touch, see or smell what "the earth's resources" are. Use a sign or gesture too for the key words (water, tree).

Answers

Try it

  • 1. True
  • 2. False
  • 3. True
  • 4. False
