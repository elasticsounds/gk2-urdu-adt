Upscaling comparison

Six pictures from pages 6, 9, 12 and 27 of the Class 2 General Knowledge book, each shown as the original scan and through eight upscalers. Tap any picture to open it full-screen, zoom in, and flip between versions at the same spot.

Keeps the pixels: Real-ESRGAN, Topaz (two modes), Recraft Crisp and Crystal enlarge and sharpen what is there. Restores with a diffusion model: Clarity adds texture while trying to stay close to the scan. (SUPIR was also tried but the hosted model failed on every picture.) Redraws: GPT Image 2.5 Flare and Nano Banana 2 regenerate the whole picture.
